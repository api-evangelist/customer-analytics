# Customer Analytics

> Customer Analytics (CA) is a data, analytics and AI consultancy headquartered in Middleton, Wisconsin, with a delivery centre in Guindy, Chennai, India. It delivers AI/ML and data analytics, data engineering, business intelligence and reporting, business solutions design and software development, platform implementation and software management, and quality assurance — plus six productised "360" offerings. Customer Analytics is a professional services firm and publishes no public API, developer portal, SDKs or machine-readable API specifications.

## Notes for agents

- There is no public API, developer portal, API reference, SDK, CLI, MCP server or machine-readable specification for Customer Analytics as of 2026-07-20.
- No /.well-known/ discovery documents are published; /.well-known/security.txt, /openid-configuration, /oauth-authorization-server, /api-catalog and /ai-plugin.json all return 404.
- The /api/ path on customeranalytics.com is disallowed in robots.txt and serves the site's own internal Next.js application endpoints; it is not a public API.
- Engagement is via the contact form or Info@customeranalytics.com, not via self-serve signup.
